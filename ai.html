My Approach

Not “AI for the sake of it”, but concrete points in the research and investing process where automation and LLMs actually change the quality and speed of decisions.

01 · Game Intake

From Games to Lessons

Turning games, puzzles, and notes into a short list of patterns worth practicing.

  • Use AI to cluster mistakes by theme: tactics, time pressure, opening gaps, endgames, and plans.
  • Flag repeated weaknesses that deserve deliberate practice.
  • Push the shortlist into Notion, spreadsheets, or a dashboard for focused training.
02 · Deep Review

Augmenting Game Analysis

Human judgment stays at the centre; AI cleans, summarizes and cross‑checks.

  • Parse 5–10 years of annual reports into structured tables and key quotes.
  • Compare engine lines, human plans, and actual moves using Python-assisted review.
  • Use LLMs to draft lessons from a game, then refine them manually.
03 · Training Loop

Always-On Improvement Loop

Once a thesis is live, the hard part is staying updated without burning out.

  • Automate alerts for macro, regulatory and company‑specific events.
  • Summarize every new game into what changed vs. previous reviews.
  • Maintain a living change log for openings, recurring mistakes, and rating goals.

Questions, Answered

A quick FAQ on how I think about AI in chess and what I can realistically help with.

Do you replace thinking with AI?

No. The goal is to remove repetitive sorting and summarizing so more energy goes into calculation, judgement, and learning from mistakes.

What kind of projects are a good fit?

Anything with repeated study data: PGNs, puzzle logs, opening notes, review checklists, or dashboards that should stay fresh with minimal manual work.

Is this useful for casual players too?

Yes. Even solo practitioners and small teams can benefit from simple automations: template‑driven client notes, standardised checklists, and semi‑automated research packs.

How do you handle data privacy and sensitive information?

Privacy is paramount. I prioritize local-first solutions where sensitive data stays on your infrastructure. When using LLMs, we use enterprise-grade APIs with zero-data retention policies to ensure your proprietary thesis never enters a training set.

Is this only for advanced chess players?

No. My focus is on fundamental analysis and medium-to-long term strategies. I build tools that help you think better and research faster, not systems that compete on millisecond execution speeds.

Do I need a team of developers to maintain this?

Not necessarily. I build these systems using stable, well-documented libraries. Most workflows can be maintained by a single operationally-focused analyst, or I can provide ongoing support as your "fractional quant."

What areas do you focus on?

My focus is chess study, AI-assisted analysis, Python tooling, web interfaces, and dashboards for tracking improvement.

Can you build chess study tools?

Yes. I can shape small tools for PGN review, opening notes, puzzle tracking, dashboards, and AI-assisted summaries.

Do you share one-off builds or ongoing experiments?

I offer both. For some, a single "infrastructure sprint" to automate a specific pain point is enough. For others, I provide ongoing support to iterate on models and keep data pipelines running smoothly as a "fractional quant."

Need a Chess-Tech Collaborator?

I build at the intersection of chess strategy and modern engineering: study tools, AI workflows, dashboards, and clean web experiences.
